# ScienceDojo Business: Pilot Offer v1

Internal offer reference, 10 October 2026. Approved service scope is consolidated here; workload and commercial viability remain under review in B3. Final project terms require Piumal's approval.

## Buyer and positioning

ScienceDojo helps English-working IT service and software implementation teams turn an established workplace process into practical onboarding. We work directly with the client's subject expert to create a focused browser module with realistic decisions, an applied exercise and a guide for reviewing the learner's work.

Target buyers are responsible for operations, service delivery, training or a small company's delivery team. The initial problem is new staff applying an agreed process inconsistently, for example an incomplete client handover. An approved process, relevant source material, one subject expert and one nominated approver are required. Learning agencies are a separate route for defined instructional-design or production support, quoted to their brief.

## Custom Onboarding Module

**From €1,490 per project** for one task, one learner group and English. Approximately 5–10 minutes is estimated total learner practice time, not the provider's development time or a measured completion result.

| Included | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Source review and briefing | Agreed pack initially capped at approximately 15 pages or 20 slides, subject to density review; one 45-minute briefing and limited written clarification. |
| Learning design | Up to three observable objectives and one reviewable storyboard based on the client's approved process. |
| Interactive practice | Up to three decisions within one focused scenario, explanatory feedback and reuse of existing components. |
| Applied assessment | One realistic task and a simple scoring guide for human review by the client's team. |
| Reviews | One consolidated storyboard review and one consolidated working-module review, supplied by the nominated approver. |
| Checks and handover | Agreed usability/browser/delivery checks, the approved design, exercise and scoring guide, setup notes and a working module in the agreed delivery route. |

A change to the approved task, audience, language, delivery route or substantive content requires a revised scope. Errors against the agreed specification are corrected rather than charged as scope additions. Dates depend on available production capacity and timely client inputs and reviews; agree them before work begins.

## Delivery and continued use

The standard delivery is a self-contained HTML browser module with readable/editable HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The client may keep and use the delivered module internally without a ScienceDojo subscription or per-learner fee, and supplies suitable hosting if required. The proposal records usage rights and any third-party licences; reusable components retain their applicable rights. The standalone web-server route has been tested. Direct opening of a saved downloaded file still needs its recorded check before that route is committed to for a client.

Managed hosting, ongoing changes/support, SCORM/LMS reporting and integrations are separately scoped. The base module has no learner accounts, central response storage, automated marking or certificates.

## Exclusions and quotation rule

The base scope excludes an entire onboarding curriculum, creation of company policies/processes, extensive research, bespoke video/animation, extra languages, custom AI/platform features and rollout support.

€1,490 is a starting-price hypothesis for a tightly controlled adaptation. The original allowance is **20 planned delivery hours plus four contingency hours**, with **€50 illustrative direct costs**.
