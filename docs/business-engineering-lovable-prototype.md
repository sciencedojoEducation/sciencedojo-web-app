## Submitted design brief

Create a polished, original, responsive visual-design prototype named "ScienceDojo Engineering — Design Exploration". This is a separate design prototype for an existing Next.js application, NOT a replacement production service or backend migration. Build a working frontend with your normal stack. Do not provision a database, authentication, paid integrations, analytics, email delivery, domain, public deployment or GitHub connection.

DESIGN GOAL
Advanced + simple. A classy, modern engineering studio for a small Sri Lankan team, with direct human collaboration. Sophisticated visual craft comparable to excellent independent studios, with clear buyer decisions and understandable content. Learn from coherent art direction and progressive explanation (Zero Gravity) and relevant proof/delivery clarity (Fcode Labs), without copying their layout, branding, text or artwork. The current page felt text-heavy, so create stronger visual storytelling and deliberate editorial rhythm. Avoid a generic SaaS template, repetitive bento/card grids, cartoonish whole-page styling, decorative underlines, highlighter strokes, doodles, fake dashboards and excessive gradients. Visually outstanding but calm, structured and readable.

BRAND
Logo MUST be the simple continuous lowercase wordmark "sciencedojo." with one trailing dot. No extra logo icon. "Engineering" is a separate descriptor. Palette: primary blue #0066FF, navy #001A44, cyan #00D2FF, white and pale blue. Navy 'science', blue 'dojo', cyan trailing dot are suitable. Typography: crisp editorial sans, excellent hierarchy, carefully composed large headline, readable body and supporting labels. Use an intentional grid, generous but controlled whitespace and varied section compositions. Mature, light visual language with one purposeful dark-navy contrast section if useful. Keep copy concise.

AUDIENCE AND OFFER
Lead with established small training providers and education teams that already have learners/courses and need a specific improvement to a website, learning platform or connected workflow. Suitable web applications and workflow projects in other sectors are welcome as a secondary route. Services stand independently of buying ScienceDojo software. Start with a focused project, or discuss ongoing development. Scope, delivery team, availability, dates, fees, source/usage arrangements, handover and support are agreed for each project. No engineering price, staffing capacity, delivery-time guarantee, 24/7 support, certified platform partnership, revenue claim, client testimonial, client logo or success metric has been established. Do not invent them. This is not an offer to replace every LMS; check native settings/integrations before proposing custom software.
