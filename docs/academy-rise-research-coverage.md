- Media: gallery/carousel blocks, image cropping and closed captions.

## Visual evidence inspected

- Official labelled blank-course editor screenshot associated with the September 2026 guide.
- Official image-crop interface screenshot.
- The two Articulate authoring screenshots supplied with the ScienceDojo request: quick-add block tray and contextual text/layout picker.

The visual evidence supports these observations: the editor uses a shallow global toolbar; the content canvas dominates the viewport; the outline is edited inline; selection is indicated sparingly; block tools are contextual; and visual presets are previews of semantic layouts rather than technical configuration forms.

## Boundaries and unresolved evidence

- The research did not sign in to a paid Articulate account or modify real Rise content.
- Tutorial videos were not all transcript-audited when the accompanying official article already provided the complete workflow. This ledger does not count them as visually inspected.
- The support guide links into Storyline, Review, Reach, account management and accessibility indexes. Those products were followed only where they clarified the Rise authoring experience.
- Template catalogue entries were sampled to understand discovery, preview and editable-copy behaviour; individual course subject matter is not interface evidence.
- Articulate's newer AI-generated formats are documented here to understand workflow separation. They are not recommendations to add AI authoring to the current ScienceDojo milestone.

## Build order supported by the research

1. Shared learner/editor rendering and a trustworthy interactive preview.
2. Stable contextual selection, settings drawers and focus management.
3. Nondestructive visual presets and real thumbnails for every exposed variant.
4. Complete media placement workflow, including crop/focal point, caption and accessibility metadata.
5. Assessment authoring with interactive preview and explicit policy settings.
6. Reusable block/lesson patterns with independent copies.
7. Only then: new interactions, question banks, collaboration, review, localization or external publishing.
