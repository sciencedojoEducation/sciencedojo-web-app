# ScienceDojo pricing and workload review

Prepared 10 October 2026 for B3. The approved website remains **from €1,490 per Custom Onboarding Module**. These calculations test that starting-price hypothesis; they do not establish market acceptance, measured production speed or a sustainable minimum.

## What the current price covers financially

The original estimate is 20 planned delivery hours plus 20% contingency, giving 24 delivery hours. The illustration also assumes four sales/proposal hours and €50 project-specific costs.

| Calculation | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| €1,490 project revenue less €50 direct costs | €1,440 |
| €1,440 divided by 24 delivery hours | €60.00 per delivery hour |
| €1,440 divided by 28 delivery plus sales hours | €51.43 per total project-related hour |
| 28 total hours at a hypothetical €60 target, plus €50 direct costs | €1,730 |
| Total hours affordable at €1,490, €50 costs and a hypothetical €60 target | 24 hours |
| The same limit after reserving four sales hours | 20 delivery hours, including contingency |

These figures precede fixed overhead and tax. They assume €1,490 is revenue available to the business before any applicable sales tax; if the headline includes VAT or another sales tax, use the net revenue instead. €1,730 is an arithmetic scenario, not an approved new price. The €60 target is provisional, not Piumal's confirmed required rate.

Sales, administration, operating costs and realistic chargeable time belong in the calculation. [IHK Munich's pricing guidance](https://www.ihk-muenchen.de/ratgeber/gruendung/preiskalkulation/) supports accounting for these costs and non-billable work; its general advice does not validate ScienceDojo's particular hours or rate. ## Stress the estimate before quoting

Illustrative changes to the estimate, not observed production results or competitor benchmarks:

| Planned delivery | Contingency at 20% | Sales hours | Total hours | After €50 direct costs at €1,490 | Hypothetical €60 target price before fixed overhead and sales tax |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 4 | 4 | 28 | €51.43/hour | €1,730 |
| 22 | 4.4 | 4 | 30.4 | €47.37/hour | €1,874 |
| 30 | 6 | 6 | 42 | €34.29/hour | €2,570 |

For example, a development estimate rising from six to eight hours raises planned delivery from 20 to 22 hours. Record the specific cause instead of adding hidden unpaid work. A client's budget may justify a smaller deliverable, such as design/storyboard support, but it does not make the full scope cheaper to produce.

## Quotation rules for owner review

1. Estimate each phase against the actual approved sources and delivery requirement. Check content density, not page count alone.
2. Include both review rounds, coordination, testing, handover and project-related sales effort. Add expected software, media or subcontracting costs rather than assuming the €50 allowance is enough.
3. Check the resulting effective return against Piumal's required economics and allocated fixed costs. A delivery-only rate is insufficient for this decision.
4. Keep €1,490 only for work that fits both the scope and the reviewed effort/cost ceiling. Otherwise reduce the agreed deliverables or quote the larger scope separately.
5. Do not include new branching architecture, unsupported authoring tools, SCORM/LMS integrations, additional languages or bespoke media in a six-hour template build.
6. Re-estimate material changes after storyboard approval. Correct errors against the agreed specification within the project; do not turn those into paid scope additions.
7. Confirm the client's actual browser/hosting route before promising compatibility. Saved-file opening remains unconfirmed; the static web-server route has been tested.
8. Agree task, approver, scope, price, tax treatment, acceptance criteria, rights, inputs and schedule before work begins. Complete R1 and verify any required delivery capability before accepting paid work.

## Inputs still needed from Piumal

The owner has confirmed an intended full-time commitment for a future start, a minimum monthly personal income target before personal tax, and an estimated monthly business-cost allowance. The private capacity/economics record retains these inputs and the conditional monthly scenarios. Numerical weekly delivery hours, start date, company funding cost of the personal target and applicable tax treatment remain unresolved; unknown calculator inputs stay blank.

- Available weekly delivery hours, separately from sales/general administration.
- Company funding required for the recorded personal income target; distinguish personal income from company turnover or retained funds without presuming a remuneration/tax route.
- A breakdown and review of the recorded monthly business-cost estimate, appropriate reserves and actual project-specific costs; avoid counting the same cost twice.
- A realistic number of productive/chargeable hours and working weeks; do not assume every available hour can be sold.
- Review of the phase estimates and the maximum acceptable effort at the starting price.
- Applicable tax treatment and whether a client quotation presents a net or tax-inclusive fee.

The editable calculator and private owner inputs are kept under the ignored `.business-private/` directory. Unknown values remain blank; filling assumptions produces a planning result, not an owner approval or legal clearance.

## How to validate the development allowance

Use the next qualified brief, or one bounded internal adaptation based on an approved example, and log source review, storyboard, template changes, assessment, reviews, checks and handover separately. Record elapsed productive effort, purchased services and avoidable rework. Do not count an automated export's run time as the complete development effort. Compare actual effort with the estimate before repeating the price.
