# German A2 course

Course key: `german-a2-complete`. Entry requirement: A1 German. The existing academy builder, required-block submissions, final assessment and exam-choice flow are reused. Course content is a new document; A1, B1 and B2 content is not changed.

The course develops connected everyday communication: describing experiences, making arrangements, solving routine problems and writing useful messages. It contains **15 thematic chapters, integrated A2 Mastery, and two separate ten-lesson exam routes**. One learner completes 26 lessons: 16 shared lessons and their chosen route. Estimated guided activity time is 32 hours for the shared course and 13.5 hours for one exam route. These estimates do not assert that all learners attain A2 in this time.

## Curriculum

Each shared chapter follows the fourteen requested phases: Einstieg, Wortschatz, Hören, Lesen, Sprache entdecken, Grammatik, Redemittel, Sprechen, Schreiben, Deutsch im Alltag, Mission, Prüfungsblick, Recall and Chapter Mastery. Activities include contextual vocabulary cards, original audio and reading, answer explanations, spoken and written submissions, and transfer decisions.

| Chapter | Communication | Grammar and connections |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · A1 → A2 bridge | Introduce oneself in connected sentences; identify learning needs | Present, accusative, dative, modals, separable verbs, Perfekt, word order and questions |
| 2 · People and relationships | Describe people and compare relationships | Possessives, accusative/dative pronouns, comparisons; und, aber, auch |
| 3 · Daily life and time | Describe routines and reschedule appointments | Reflexive/separable verbs, time expressions and frequency; zuerst, dann, danach |
| 4 · Past experiences | Narrate a weekend with a problem and solution | Perfekt with haben/sein, irregular participles, inseparable verbs, war/hatte; später, zum Schluss |
| 5 · Home and neighbours | Read housing advertisements and arrange a viewing | Two-way prepositions, place/direction, stellen/stehen, legen/liegen, dative verbs |
| 6 · Food and healthy living | Order food, clarify ingredients, explain a recipe | Imperatives for du/ihr/Sie, quantities, advice with sollen; lieber, denn |
| 7 · Shopping and money | Compare products and request an exchange | Comparative/superlative, common adjective endings, demonstratives; sondern |
| 8 · Town and services | Ask directions and arrange service appointments | Dative prepositions, polite könnte/würde, introductory indirect questions |
| 9 · Travel and holidays | Plan a journey and respond to an announcement | Perfekt review, present/future meaning, werden introduction; wenn, deshalb/deswegen |
| 10 · Work | Describe duties, respond to shift changes, write a simple application | Common modal Präteritum, formal register; weil |
| 11 · School and languages | Explain goals and choose a course | weil/dass, verb-final clauses, introductory zu infinitives and prepositional verbs |
| 12 · Health | Describe symptoms, arrange an appointment, notify work | sollen/müssen/dürfen, nicht dürfen vs nicht müssen, seit and reflexive review |
| 13 · Free time and media | Respond to invitations and negotiate activities | Prepositional verbs, proposals and conditional review; außerdem |
| 14 · Communication and problems | Explain a delivery problem and request a solution | Polite requests, pronouns and clause consolidation; sondern, deswegen |
| 15 · Plans, opinions and life in Germany | Give reasons and plan a community activity | Future meaning, comparisons, reasons/results/conditions; relative structures only as a preview |
| 16 · A2 Mastery | Integrated housing, journey, health and order missions | Choose structures by communicative purpose; react to changed information |

Writing moves from personal messages to invitations, requests, appointment changes, simple formal applications and complaints. Course models fit the displayed word ranges. Every chapter contains four speaking modes: **Answer, Ask, Describe, Interact**. Short model answers demonstrate structure; learners extend them with their own information and spontaneous responses.

Every chapter has a **Meine Redemittel** writing activity. It saves a personal phrase entry through existing learner submissions, accessible again in that lesson; it is not a separate global phrasebook feature. Recall revisits current vocabulary and material from approximately one, three and seven chapters earlier. The three-day/one-week schedule is learner-led rather than an automated reminder system.

There are 128 contextual vocabulary cards across the shared chapters, supplemented by the reading, listening and phrase activities. This is a focused course lexicon building on A1, not a claim to exhaust Goethe's approximately 1,300-entry reference. The official wordlist is linked for systematic gap review and extension.

Metadata on each block records CEFR A2, domain, chapter topic, skills, communicative function, grammar context and exam route where applicable. Topic grammar tags describe instruction, not a computed learner mastery score. The current schema does not include a separate A2.1/A2.2 difficulty field.

## Diagnosis, mastery and progression

The bridge has seven non-gating grammar checks and separate reading/listening checks and productive submissions. Learners record weaknesses and use the provided remediation map. This is not an adaptive diagnostic or an automatic skill profile.

A2 Mastery includes four additional integrated missions. Each has an original reading source, new spoken information, a supported decision, a written response and a speaking interaction. Missions require learners to notice changed times, availability, constraints or product details. The shared 18-question assessment contains six reading, six listening and six language questions. Reading evidence is included in each assessment prompt; listening evidence is accessible through the linked audio and transcript.

The existing navigation requires shared chapters and the internal assessment before exam selection. Only the selected ten-lesson branch is then required. A diagnostic bypass for learners already at A2 is **not implemented**, because the existing academy does not provide that entry flow. The 70% internal pass mark is a course navigation threshold, not an official exam score or guarantee. Writing and speaking need checklist review and preferably feedback from a teacher.

## Exam preparation

Both routes contain ten stages: know your exam, reading, listening, writing, speaking, strategies/common mistakes, Mini-Mock 1, personal error review, Mini-Mock 2 and official practice. Mini-mocks use new inputs compared with the intensive exercises. They are explicitly reduced skill simulations, not full official-length exams. Matching tasks use choice controls; telephone notes and form fields use free written submissions. External timers and learner-controlled playback are used; no exam timer or playback limit is claimed.

**Goethe-Zertifikat A2 for adults:** reading about 30 minutes with four parts; listening about 30 minutes with four parts; writing 30 minutes (SMS 20–30 words, E-Mail 30–40 words); speaking about 15 minutes with questions about oneself, an account of one's life and collaborative scheduling. Hearing playback practice follows the model: parts 1/4 twice, parts 2/3 once. [Official Goethe information](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/prf/gzsd2/wi2.html), [Goethe model writing](https://bfu.goethe.de/a2_mod_2MX5/schreiben.php), [Goethe model listening](https://bfu.goethe.de/a2_mod_2MX5/hoeren.php), [Goethe model speaking](https://bfu.goethe.de/a2_mod_2MX5/sprechen.php).

**Start Deutsch 2 / telc Deutsch A2:** listening about 20 minutes with three parts; reading (three parts) and writing (two parts) share 50 minutes; speaking about 15 minutes, usually two participants, no preparation time. Writing includes a five-field form and a personal message of about 40 words selecting three of four points in the current model. Listening trains telephone notes (twice), short-text choice (once), and conversation matching (twice). Speaking trains introduction, everyday conversation and negotiation. This is the general A2 exam, not A2+ Beruf or a B1 exam. [Official telc information and downloadable practice pack](https://www.telc.net/sprachpruefungen/deutsch/start-deutsch-2-telc-deutsch-a2/).

Formats verified on **30 September 2026** using the official Goethe adult model and telc's downloadable `20241212_5090-B00-010107` practice booklet. All ScienceDojo teaching/test texts are original; official tests are linked and not republished in the course.

## Content and authoring

- `lib/german-a2-curriculum.ts`: 16 chapter datasets and official sources.
- `lib/german-a2-course.ts`: academy document, fourteen-phase chapters, provider branches and final assessment.
- `lib/german-a2-exam-practice.ts`: intensive exercises and two fresh mini-mocks.
- `lib/german-a2-mastery.ts`: four integrated transfer missions.
- `public/audio/german-a2/`: 32 German recordings (16 chapters, four mastery missions, twelve exam recordings). Exam dialogues alternate German voices. Synthetic audio is labelled; transcripts are available for review.

`npm run academy:german-a2:audio` uses installed macOS German voices and encodes AAC at 64 kbps in M4A for browser playback. Existing assets are preserved unless `--force` is requested. `--start` and `--count` support partial generation. `npm run academy:german-a2:seed` validates content/audio and previews the database state. Add `-- --apply` to create a draft and register content-addressed audio in academy storage. Existing draft updates require `--update` and the fresh hash from the preview; published courses cannot be overwritten by this script. Successful updates snapshot the previous draft and check its revision.

Admin editor: `/dashboard/admin/academy/german-a2-complete`. Preview: `/dashboard/admin/academy/german-a2-complete/preview`. Normal academy publication controls learner visibility.
